Shared Strategies

Accepted by CHIP on October 1, 2026.

Current behavior

Strategies are universal and available to all users. A Strategy has no owning User and no user_id. Strategy discovery and execution do not filter by user ownership. This applies to the MVP Random Strategy and strategies created through the MVP strategy API.

Use the Strategy fields in the MVP ERD. Users continue to own their simulations and Runs as specified in the MVP design; sharing a Strategy does not change Run ownership.

Future scope

Custom strategies owned by individual users may be designed later. Do not add per-user strategy ownership, visibility controls, or a custom-strategy ownership model in the current implementation.

Design precedence

This subsequent document supersedes the Strategy ownership statements and Strategy.user_id in section 3 of the MVP design. All other MVP and ingress design requirements remain in effect.
